Tips & settings
  • Use headphones if you can. Without them, the mic can pick up VoiceCart's own reply from your speakers and mistake it for you interrupting.
  • VoiceCart asks before anything it isn't sure about ("Amul or Nandini?") and always confirms before checkout.
  • The server also detects a long pause on its own, but tapping Done talking is faster.
