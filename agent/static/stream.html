VoiceCart — streaming

Click start, then talk. Click "Done talking" after each question to get a reply right away — the server also auto-detects a long pause, but don't rely on that. You can also just start talking while a reply is playing to interrupt it. Click stop when you're done for good.

Use headphones if you can. Without them, the mic can pick up the assistant's own reply coming out of your speakers and mistake it for you interrupting.
